Last updated: 22-01-2026

Digital Product Policy

All products sold on Stribble are non-tangible, irrevocable digital goods delivered in PDF format.

Refund Policy

Refunds are not generally applicable once the digital product has been successfully delivered or downloaded. This is because digital content cannot be returned after access has been granted.

Exceptions & Processing Timeline

However, in exceptional cases (such as duplicate payments for the same order due to technical error), refunds may be considered.

If approved, the refund will be processed within 5-7 working days and credited back to the original payment method.

Cancellation Policy

Orders cannot be cancelled once payment has been completed and access to the digital product has been provided.

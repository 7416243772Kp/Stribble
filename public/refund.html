Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last Updated: 4 February 2026

At Stribble, we are committed to providing high-quality educational content. We want you to feel comfortable and confident when enrolling in our courses. That is why we offer a comprehensive 7-Day Money-Back Guarantee.

1. The 7-Day Guarantee

If you are not satisfied with a course you purchased, you may request a full refund within 7 days of the purchase date. No complex questions asked—if the content didn't provide the value you expected, we will refund your money.

2. Eligibility Conditions

To be eligible for a refund, the following conditions must be met:

  • The request must be submitted within 7 days of the original purchase date.
  • The purchase must have been made directly through the Stribble website.
  • You must not have violated our Terms of Service (e.g., attempting to share accounts or scrape content).
Please Note: Upon processing your refund, your access to the course content will be immediately revoked. You will no longer be able to view or stream the course materials in your student dashboard.

3. How to Request a Refund

To initiate a refund, please contact our support team with your Order ID and registered email address.

How to find your Order ID:
1. Go to My Courses and click on "📜 Orders" to see your history.
2. If you still cannot find it, please email us with the email address used for purchase.
  • Email: support@stribble.site
  • Subject Line: Refund Request - [Your Order ID]

Our team will review your request and process it within 24-48 hours.

4. Refund Processing Timeline

Once your refund is approved/processed by our team:

  • The funds will be reversed to your original payment method (Credit Card, Debit Card, UPI, etc.).
  • It typically takes 5-7 business days for the amount to reflect in your bank account, depending on your bank's processing speed.

5. Duplicate Payments

If you were charged twice for the same transaction due to a technical error, the duplicate transaction will be refunded immediately upon verification. Please contact us immediately if you notice a double charge.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this policy, please contact us at support@stribble.site.

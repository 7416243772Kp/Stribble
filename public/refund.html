Stribble currently offers FULL REFUNDS within 7 days of purchase subject to the conditions outlined in this policy.

1. Refund Policy Overview

At Stribble, we strive to maintain a fair and transparent refund process for all users.

Since our platform provides secure streaming-based educational access instead of downloadable products, eligible users may request refunds within the permitted refund period.

Refunds are governed by:

  • The platform Terms & Conditions
  • Applicable Indian laws
  • Payment gateway regulations
  • Fraud prevention requirements

2. Refund Eligibility

Users are eligible for a full refund if:

  • The refund request is submitted within 7 days from purchase
  • The transaction is valid and verified
  • The request does not violate platform policies

Refund requests are generally accepted without requiring detailed explanations during the eligible refund period.

Stribble currently provides full refunds within the approved refund period because courses are streamed securely and are not downloadable.

3. Refund Time Window

Refund requests must be submitted within:

7 Days from the original purchase date.

Refund requests submitted after the 7-day refund window may not be eligible.

The refund period begins from the exact date and time the successful payment is completed.

4. Refund Request Process

To request a refund, users may:

  • Contact platform support via email
  • Submit a support ticket through the platform

Refund requests should include:

  • Registered email address
  • Purchase details
  • Order ID if available

Stribble may review account activity and transaction records before approving refunds.

5. Course Access During Refund Review

Users may continue accessing their purchased courses while the refund request is under review.

However, access may be temporarily restricted if:

  • Suspicious activity is detected
  • Fraud investigation is initiated
  • Refund abuse is suspected
  • Account sharing violations occur

6. Course Access Revocation After Refund

Once a refund is approved and successfully processed:

  • Course access will be permanently revoked
  • Reading progress may be removed
  • Platform access to refunded content may be terminated

Refunded users no longer retain rights to continue accessing the refunded educational content.

7. Duplicate Payments & Accidental Transactions

If duplicate payments occur due to technical errors or accidental repeated transactions, Stribble may provide refunds even beyond the normal 7-day refund window after verification.

Verification may include:

  • Payment gateway logs
  • Order records
  • Transaction timestamps
  • Account purchase history

8. Refund Abuse, Fraud & Blacklisting

Stribble reserves the right to investigate suspicious refund activity.

Examples of suspicious activity may include:

  • Repeated purchase-and-refund patterns
  • Fraudulent payment behavior
  • Account sharing during refund periods
  • Content leakage or piracy
  • Abuse of platform systems
Accounts suspected of refund abuse may be temporarily blacklisted, restricted, permanently suspended, or denied future refunds.

During investigation periods:

  • Course access may be restricted
  • Refund processing may be delayed
  • Additional verification may be requested

9. Refund Processing Time

After a refund is approved, the processing time depends on:

  • Bank processing systems
  • Payment gateway settlement timelines
  • National holidays
  • UPI/banking infrastructure

Estimated refund processing timelines:

Typically 3–7 business days, excluding bank holidays.

Some financial institutions may require additional time.

10. Refund Payment Method

Refunds are generally processed back to the original payment source used during purchase.

Examples:

  • UPI payments → refunded to same UPI account
  • Credit/Debit cards → refunded to same card
  • Wallet payments → refunded to same wallet provider

Stribble does not guarantee alternate refund methods unless required by law or payment gateway restrictions.

11. Transaction Fees & Charges

Stribble currently bears applicable refund-related transaction charges where required.

Eligible users generally receive the complete approved refund amount unless otherwise required by payment gateway rules or applicable laws.

12. Technical Issues & Platform Errors

Refunds may also be considered for genuine technical issues including:

  • Failed access after successful payment
  • Duplicate transaction errors
  • Payment confirmation failures
  • Severe platform-side delivery problems

Users are encouraged to contact support before initiating payment disputes with banks or payment providers.

13. Chargebacks & Payment Disputes

Users may raise legitimate payment disputes through official banking or payment channels where necessary.

However, fraudulent or abusive chargebacks may result in:

  • Permanent account suspension
  • Restriction from future purchases
  • Investigation for fraud or abuse
  • Legal action where applicable

Stribble reserves the right to present transaction logs, access records, platform activity, and usage data during dispute investigations.

14. Exceptions & Limitations

Refunds may be denied in situations including but not limited to:

  • Refund request after the 7-day window
  • Fraudulent activity
  • Account sharing violations
  • Piracy or content leakage
  • Abuse of refund systems
  • Violation of Terms & Conditions
Stribble reserves the right to investigate any suspicious behavior before processing refunds.

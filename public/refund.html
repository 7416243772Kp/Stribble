Refund and Cancellation Policy

At Stribble, we offer digital products (Ebooks and Courses). Due to the nature of digital content, please read our policy carefully:

1. Cancellations

Orders for digital products cannot be cancelled once the payment is processed and the download link/access is provided.

2. Refunds

Since our products are digital assets that are delivered instantly via email, we generally **do not offer refunds** once the purchase is complete. However, a refund may be considered if:

  • The payment was successful but you did not receive the product within 24 hours.
  • There was a duplicate payment due to a technical error.

3. Processing Refunds

If a refund is approved, it will be processed and automatically applied to your original method of payment within 5-7 working days as per Razorpay and bank standards.

4. Contact

For any refund-related queries, please contact us at support@stribble.com with your Order ID.

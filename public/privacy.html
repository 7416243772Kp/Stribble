By using Stribble, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this Privacy Policy and consent to the collection and use of your information as described below.

1. Acceptance of Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy applies to all users, visitors, customers, affiliates, and anyone interacting with the Stribble platform.

By accessing or using Stribble, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

2. Information We Collect

Stribble may collect personal and technical information including:

  • Full name
  • Email address
  • Encrypted authentication credentials
  • Course purchase history
  • Course reading progress
  • Support tickets and messages
  • Review submissions
  • Uploaded support attachments/images
  • Login session information
  • Payment transaction references
  • OTP verification data

We may also collect technical and usage-related information necessary for maintaining platform functionality, security, and performance.

Stribble does NOT directly store raw credit card numbers, debit card information, or sensitive banking credentials.

3. How We Use Your Data

We use collected information to:

  • Create and manage user accounts
  • Provide course access
  • Process purchases and refunds
  • Authenticate users securely
  • Monitor course engagement and reading progress
  • Respond to support requests
  • Improve platform stability and user experience
  • Protect against fraud, abuse, and piracy
  • Send transactional and marketing communications
  • Maintain legal and financial records

4. Cookies & Tracking Technologies

Stribble uses cookies and similar technologies to improve functionality, maintain sessions, and enhance user experience.

Cookies may be used for:

  • User authentication
  • Session management
  • Security verification
  • Platform preferences
  • Analytics and performance monitoring

Users may disable cookies through browser settings; however, certain platform features may stop functioning properly.

5. Analytics & Performance Monitoring

Stribble uses analytics and infrastructure monitoring systems to understand:

  • Website traffic
  • Page performance
  • Platform speed
  • General user interaction
  • Website stability

We currently use services such as:

  • Google Analytics
  • Cloudflare

These tools help improve platform performance, content delivery, caching efficiency, and protection against malicious traffic.

6. Payment Information

All payments are processed securely through Cashfree and its supported payment systems.

Stribble does not directly process or store:

  • Card numbers
  • CVV details
  • Net banking credentials
  • UPI PINs

Payment processors handle these details within their own secure environments.

We may store limited transactional references such as:

  • Order IDs
  • Payment status
  • Transaction references
  • Purchase timestamps

7. Data Security & Encryption

Stribble implements multiple security measures to protect platform data and user information.

Password Protection

User passwords are never stored in plain text. Passwords are irreversibly hashed before storage.

Encrypted Financial Information

Sensitive payout routing information used for promoter commission systems is encrypted at the application level.

Authentication Protection

Session data, OTP systems, and authentication mechanisms are secured using cryptographic protections.

Infrastructure Security

Stribble uses security systems including:

  • DDoS protection
  • Rate limiting
  • NoSQL injection prevention
  • Script injection protection
  • Session validation systems
While we implement strong security practices, no online platform can guarantee absolute security against every possible cyber threat.

8. Third-Party Data Sharing & Disclosure

Stribble does not sell, rent, or trade your personal information.

However, certain limited information may be shared with trusted third-party providers for platform operation purposes.

Payment Processors

Necessary transactional information may be securely shared with Cashfree for payment processing, refund handling, and promoter payout systems.

Email Infrastructure Providers

We use SMTP2GO for sending transactional and marketing emails including:

  • Password reset emails
  • Purchase confirmations
  • Refund notifications
  • Platform announcements

Analytics Providers

Cloudflare and Google Analytics may receive limited technical or aggregated analytical information to improve platform performance.

Legal Authorities

We may disclose information if legally required by:

  • Court orders
  • Government authorities
  • Law enforcement agencies
  • Legal investigations

We may also disclose information to investigate fraud, piracy, abuse, or violations of our Terms & Conditions.

9. Emails & Communications

Stribble sends two categories of emails:

Transactional Emails

  • OTP verification
  • Password resets
  • Purchase confirmations
  • Refund notifications
  • Security notifications

These emails are necessary for platform operation and cannot generally be unsubscribed from while using the service.

Marketing & Promotional Emails

  • Learning tips
  • Course-related educational suggestions
  • Platform announcements
  • Educational updates

Users may unsubscribe from marketing communications using the unsubscribe link provided in those emails.

10. Data Retention

Stribble may retain user information for as long as the platform continues operating or as required for:

  • Legal compliance
  • Tax obligations
  • Fraud prevention
  • Dispute handling
  • Security investigations
  • Financial auditing

Certain transactional records may be retained even after account deletion where legally required.

11. User Rights Regarding Personal Data

Data Access Requests

Users may request a copy of their stored personal data including:

  • Account information
  • Course history
  • Interaction records

Requests can be made by contacting: support@stribble.site

Account & Data Deletion Requests

Users may request account deletion by contacting: support@stribble.site

Subject Line: Data Deletion Request

Upon deletion:

  • Course access may be permanently revoked
  • General profile information may be removed or anonymized
  • Certain legal and financial records may still be retained
Certain records such as transaction logs, purchase history, payout records, and legal documentation may be retained for tax compliance, anti-fraud protection, and auditing obligations.

12. Children's Privacy

Users under 18 years of age may use the platform only with parental or guardian consent.

Parents or guardians are responsible for monitoring the minor’s use of the platform.

13. Reviews, Support Messages & Attachments

Users may voluntarily submit:

  • Course reviews
  • Support tickets
  • Contact form submissions
  • Images and attachments

Users are responsible for ensuring that submitted content:

  • Does not violate laws
  • Does not contain malware
  • Does not infringe copyrights
  • Does not contain abusive or illegal content

14. Security & Anti-Piracy Monitoring

To protect educational content and platform integrity, Stribble may implement security measures including:

  • Session restriction systems
  • Inactive tab detection
  • Blur protection on inactive windows
  • Text selection restrictions
  • Right-click disabling
  • Watermark/email visibility systems

These systems are intended solely for platform protection, copyright enforcement, and anti-piracy monitoring.

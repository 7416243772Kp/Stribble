Privacy Policy

Last updated: 4 February 2026

Welcome to Stribble. We are committed to protecting your personal data. This policy explains how we handle your information when you use our secure course platform.

1. Information We Collect

We collect the following types of information:

  • Account Information: When you login via Google, we collect your Name, Email Address, and Google Profile ID.
  • Transaction Data: We store Order IDs and payment status (processed securely via Razorpay). We do not store card numbers or UPI PINs.
  • Usage Data: We log IP addresses and login timestamps to enforce our "Single Session" security policy.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • Access Control: To verify your purchase and grant access to the secure course reader.
  • Security & Watermarking: Your Email Address and Order ID are used to generate a dynamic, personal watermark on the course pages you view. This is a security feature to prevent piracy.
  • Communication: To send payment receipts, refund notifications, and important account updates.

3. Cookies & Session Storage

We use HTTP cookies to manage your login session.

  • Essential Cookies: These are required to keep you logged in securely and prevent concurrent sessions on multiple devices.
  • Analytics: We may use anonymous analytics to improve platform performance.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. We only share data with:

  • Payment Processors (Razorpay): Strictly for processing payments and refunds.
  • Legal Authorities: If required by law or to protect our rights in cases of fraud or piracy.

5. Your Rights

You have the right to request the deletion of your account. However, please note that deleting your account will result in the permanent loss of access to all purchased courses.

All products sold on Stribble are delivered digitally through a secure streaming-based educational access system. No physical shipping or downloadable PDF delivery is provided.

1. Delivery Overview

Stribble is a digital educational platform that provides secure access to educational courses after successful payment confirmation.

All course materials are delivered electronically through the Stribble platform itself and are accessible through the user account dashboard.

We do NOT provide:

  • Physical product shipping
  • Printed materials
  • Downloadable PDFs
  • Email downloadable attachments
  • USB/DVD deliveries

2. Delivery Method

Courses are delivered exclusively through a secure streaming-based reading system integrated within the Stribble platform.

After successful payment:

  • The purchased course is linked to the user's account
  • The course becomes available inside the dashboard
  • The user may access the course through the "My Courses" section

Users must login using their registered account credentials to access purchased courses.

3. Course Access Duration

Purchased courses are generally accessible for the lifetime of the platform unless:

  • The user violates platform policies
  • Refunds are processed
  • Accounts are suspended
  • Access is revoked for legal or security reasons
"Lifetime access" means access remains available as long as the Stribble platform and related services continue operating.

4. Delivery Timing

Course access is generally granted instantly after successful payment confirmation.

In most situations:

  • Access is activated immediately
  • Users may start reading instantly
  • No manual approval is required

However, delays may occasionally occur due to:

  • Payment gateway verification
  • Bank settlement delays
  • Server maintenance
  • Fraud review systems
  • Unexpected technical issues

5. User Dashboard & My Courses Access

Users can access purchased courses through their dedicated dashboard section commonly referred to as:

"My Courses"

The dashboard may display:

  • Purchased courses
  • Reading progress
  • Course continuation status
  • Recent activity

Users are responsible for maintaining access to their registered email account and login credentials.

6. Secure Streaming-Based Course System

Stribble uses a secure streaming-style educational access system instead of downloadable course distribution.

This system is designed to:

  • Protect educational content
  • Reduce piracy
  • Prevent unauthorized sharing
  • Maintain platform integrity

Users may fully view and read purchased courses but are not permitted to:

  • Download content
  • Copy platform materials
  • Redistribute content
  • Upload content elsewhere
  • Share protected materials publicly
Purchasing a course grants access rights only and does NOT transfer ownership or intellectual property rights to the user.

7. Access Restrictions & Platform Controls

To protect educational content and platform security, Stribble may implement restrictions including:

  • Right-click disabling
  • Text selection prevention
  • Inactive tab blur systems
  • Session-based restrictions
  • Email watermark visibility
  • Unauthorized access monitoring

Attempts to bypass these protections may result in:

  • Access suspension
  • Permanent bans
  • Refund denial
  • Legal action

8. Multi-Device Access Policy

Stribble generally allows only one active device or browser session at a time per account.

If another login attempt is detected:

  • The currently active session information may be displayed
  • Access from the new device may be blocked
  • The user may need to logout from the previously active device manually

Repeated unauthorized multi-device access attempts may trigger:

  • Temporary account restrictions
  • Security reviews
  • Suspicious activity investigation
  • Permanent suspension in severe cases

9. Technical Requirements

To access courses successfully, users generally require:

  • A stable internet connection
  • A supported browser
  • Valid account credentials
  • Access to their registered email address

Since courses are streamed digitally, internet access is required for viewing course content.

Offline access is currently not supported.

10. Platform Security & Protection Systems

Stribble uses infrastructure protection systems to maintain platform stability and security.

Security systems may include:

  • DDoS protection
  • Rate limiting
  • Session validation
  • Request filtering
  • Access monitoring
  • Anti-abuse systems

These systems are intended to protect:

  • User accounts
  • Educational content
  • Payment systems
  • Platform availability

11. Delivery Delays & Interruptions

Although Stribble aims to provide uninterrupted access, delays or interruptions may occur because of:

  • Server maintenance
  • Internet outages
  • Payment gateway issues
  • Cloud infrastructure problems
  • Cybersecurity incidents
  • Unexpected technical failures

Stribble does not guarantee uninterrupted or error-free access at all times.

Temporary downtime for maintenance, security upgrades, or infrastructure protection may occur without prior notice.

12. Delivery & Access Support

Users experiencing course access issues may contact platform support.

Support requests may include:

  • Missing course access
  • Payment-related access delays
  • Login issues
  • Dashboard problems
  • OTP delivery issues

Support channels currently include:

  • Email support
  • Ticket support system
Support requests can be submitted 24/7. However, active support monitoring generally occurs between 6:00 AM and 10:00 PM IST.

Typical response times may range between:

  • 2–30 minutes under normal conditions
  • Up to 4 days during high-demand periods or exceptional situations

13. Access Revocation & Enforcement

Stribble reserves the right to revoke course access immediately in situations involving:

  • Refund completion
  • Account sharing
  • Fraudulent behavior
  • Piracy attempts
  • Terms & Conditions violations
  • Platform abuse
  • Legal violations
Serious violations involving piracy, unauthorized sharing, defamation, or malicious misuse may lead to legal proceedings and permanent blacklisting.

14. Service Limitations & Disclaimer

Stribble does not guarantee:

  • Continuous uninterrupted access
  • Compatibility with all devices
  • Error-free performance at all times
  • Permanent availability of every platform feature

Platform functionality may evolve over time due to:

  • Security improvements
  • Technical upgrades
  • Infrastructure changes
  • Legal compliance requirements

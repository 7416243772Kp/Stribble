INSTANT DIGITAL DELIVERY

At Stribble, all our products are digital educational courses. Upon successful completion of your payment, you will receive instant access to your purchased course(s). There is no physical shipping involved, and no waiting period for delivery.

HOW IT WORKS

Once your payment is processed and confirmed:

  • Your purchased course will be immediately available in your account dashboard.
  • You can access the course content directly through our secure, streaming-based viewer on the Stribble platform.
  • Course access is available 24/7 from any device with an internet connection.
  • There is no download required—all content is streamed securely within the website.

ACCESS DURATION

All course purchases on Stribble include lifetime access. Once you purchase a course, you can revisit and review the content at any time, as many times as you need, without any additional fees or time restrictions.

DELIVERY ISSUES

If you experience any issues accessing your purchased course after payment confirmation, please contact our support team immediately at support@stribble.site. We respond to all support queries within 24–48 hours and will work promptly to resolve any access-related concerns.
